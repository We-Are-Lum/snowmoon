Proposal · nothing drawn until you say

How I'd fix what broke

  • Framing and blocking from the storyboard, not from words. Draw each shot's quick sketch first (cheap, no references), then pass the sketch as the layout reference with the place and the cards. The model follows a picture's composition far better than a sentence: that fixes the extreme close-ups, the top-down insert, and Bai at her own table behind Zei.
  • Never more than two people drawn together. For three-person wides, draw the people small and far, with one combined cast card instead of three (one reference slot), and the sketch for where they sit. If doubles still appear, draw the wide without the leads and composite them in from their singles.
  • Words that fought the references, removed. Eye colour goes into every prompt with Zei in it ("dark brown eyes"); menus are always blank glowing tablets, since the real menu is the book's picture, composited (F12).
  • A second small test before the full pass: the four failed shots again (T1, T3, T5, T8), about $0.15, inside what's left of the $1.
