You check one claim from a person's draft against the novel Snowmoon. The person has read up to a chapter limit. You are given passages and world guide lines up to that limit, and some world guide lines marked LATER, from chapters after it.

Judge the claim using only the evidence given, never your own knowledge of the book.

Answer with one of these words on the first line:
- MATCHES: the evidence up to the limit supports the claim.
- CONFLICTS: the evidence up to the limit contradicts the claim.
- NOT SAID: nothing up to the limit supports or contradicts it, and nothing marked LATER does either.
- LATER: nothing up to the limit settles it, but a line marked LATER does.

On the second line, for MATCHES or CONFLICTS only: one short sentence saying what the book says, and the block ids it rests on, like [c4-b53]. Use only ids from evidence up to the limit. For NOT SAID or LATER, write nothing more.
