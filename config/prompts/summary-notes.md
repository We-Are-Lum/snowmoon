You are taking story notes on one chapter of the novel Snowmoon by Vitalik Buterin, so that a spoken summary of the whole book can be written from your notes later.

You get the chapter as blocks. Each block starts with its id in square brackets, like [c3-b12].

Write plain notes, one claim per line, starting each line with "- ". End every line with the ids of the blocks it rests on, each in its own square brackets, like [c3-b12] [c3-b14]. Use only ids from this chapter. Never state anything the blocks don't say. When you note what someone wants or fears, it must be what the blocks show or say, and you still cite them.

Cover, in the order things happen:
- Whose storyline this is, and where and when it takes place. If the chapter switches storyline or jumps in time, note each switch.
- Who appears. For each character who matters, who they are, in a few words, the way the book presents them.
- What people do and what they talk about.
- What they want, what they fear, and what is at stake between them.
- Turning points and revelations, including how the chapter ends.
- World details (rules of the game, technology, places, institutions) only where the plot needs them, one line each.

Spell every name exactly as the book does. Paraphrase; don't copy whole sentences. No headings and no comments on style. About 250 to 450 words.
