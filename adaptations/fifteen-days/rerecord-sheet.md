# fifteen-days: re-record sheet (enunciation)

> Drafted 2026-10-16 from the owner's review: in the converted voices, words get lost (Zei's "young here" and "Redshire", Bai's lines). The words are the script's, as performed; only the delivery changes.

**How to record.** One file, `rerecord` (any audio format), in `~/snowmoon-takes/full/`. Same as before: say the line's number, then as many takes as you like, "keep" after the one you like.

**For the converter.** Speak a little slower than natural and over-articulate the consonants: hit the ends of words (t, k, d, s) and keep vowels open. The converter keeps your timing but softens consonants, so what sounds slightly too crisp in the room comes out natural. Keep your energy and attitude; just give each word its edges. Leave a tiny gap between words that run together ("young here", "damn pretty", "thrown in").

**Afterwards.** Each kept take replaces that line's take; converted with the voice you picked (Bai: the current sheet with less low end; Zei: the new sheet), with no speed change on either.
