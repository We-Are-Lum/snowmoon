# Seed brief: DOG at dawn

> **Full spoilers** for chapters 12–31.

**The adaptation:** the dawn counterattack of chapter 30, told through Deluin's voice. The book carries that voice in four forms: his letter (c19-b153), his messages during the battle (c29-b114, c30-b4, c30-b29, c30-b61), his spoken account on the mountain (c31-b10–c31-b63), and his earlier conversations with Zei (c12, c14, c15).

**Source:** the committed chapter text in `content/snowmoon/text/`. Block IDs are `c<chapter>-b<idx>`. Dates are from the datelines: the battle runs from the evening of 3725 Snowmoon 29 (c29-b1) through Snowmoon 30 (c29-b54, c30-b1). The mountain meeting is 3725 Rainmoon 2 (c31-b1), "two days" after the battle by Zei's count (c31-b27).

## 2. What the text gives and what an adapter must invent

### Given

- The full text of every battle message Deluin sends (c29-b114, c30-b4, c30-b29, c30-b61). The exception is the coordinates message, which is received (c30-b64) but not shown.
- The order of events and most timings on Zei's side: launch at sundown (c29-b36), midnight (c29-b55), the night retreat (c30-b2), patterns shipped near sunrise (c30-b37), access cut after fifteen minutes (c30-b57), encirclement a longhour after sunrise (c30-b72), quiet by late afternoon (c30-b75).
- The casualty ratios: above 10:1 (c30-b48), then 6:1 (c30-b54), then 1.5:1 (c30-b57).
- What Deluin says he is: one of the Arctic commanders, heavily surveilled, controlling the southern flank (c29-b114).
- What Deluin can see: the queries against the simulator (c30-b29).
- His own later summary of how he rose: c31-b22–c31-b37.

### To invent

- **Deluin's physical position during the battle.** The text never places him. He says he controls the southern flank (c29-b114) and is "heavily surveilled". Nothing says where he sits, who sits with him, or how he types without being seen.
- **How he sends each message.** The text gives the channel (c29-b140) but not the act.
- **The coordinates message (c30-b64).** Its words, and what sending "exact coordinates of some of our units" (c30-b61) means for the Arctic units and people under his command. Arctic human soldiers are on the field (c30-b59).
- **The Arctic side of the dawn.** What the Arctic drones and operators see when the DOG patterns arrive, and who cuts off the simulator (c30-b57).
- **Whether Deluin knows that Den has died.** The base is destroyed just before his first message (c29-b108, c29-b111).
- **How Deluin gets from Arctic commander to the mountain stair two days later (c31-b5).** The book never shows him leave, defect, or be recovered. His mountain account covers Grapetime to his promotion (c31-b22–c31-b34) and stops before the battle.
- **His parents during and after the battle.** They were offered a Glasscrown post (c31-b23) and were bound for Arctic core territory (c19-b153). They are not mentioned after c31-b23.

## 5. Open creative questions

1. **Where does Deluin's voice live?** It could stay in the forms the book gives (letter, messages, retelling), with the night shown only through what he writes. Or the adaptation could put him on the page at his Arctic post, inventing the room, the watchers and the act of sending (see section 2).
2. **What does the dawn cost him?** He sends his own units' coordinates (c30-b61, c30-b64), and Arctic people die on the field (c30-b59, c30-b75). How much of that does Deluin see, and does he say anything about it? The book has him say nothing about it.
3. **How does it end?** The book jumps from "jie fe hen dzi" (c30-b61) to the sweater on the mountain stair (c31-b5). The simulator was cut off (c30-b57) while he was the one feeding it. Was he suspected, and how did he get out?

## 6. Suggested length and beats

*Suggestion only.* A short story of 3,500–5,000 words, or a 12–15 minute audio piece in Deluin's first person. Where the book gives his words, quote them verbatim.

1. **Prologue, Grapetime.** The letter written in a non-transmitting room with stolen watches (c19-b153).
2. **Snowmoon 29, sundown.** The Veridian launch, seen from the Arctic side (c29-b50–c29-b52). (The Arctic side is invented.)
3. **Night.** Rear base #3 struck (c29-b108). He sends the southern-flank message (c29-b114).
4. **Zei's reply asks for the raw weights** (c29-b140). He can't send them; he sends simulator keys and proxies instead (c30-b4).
5. **The queries start.** He sees them and warns about the sentries (c30-b28–c30-b29).
6. **Dawn.** DOG on every drone (c30-b47). The breakthrough at more than ten to one (c30-b48).
7. **Access cut** (c30-b57). The danger to his cover. (Invented.)
8. **"I hope you trust me now. … jie fe hen dzi."** (c30-b61). The coordinates (c30-b64).
9. **Coda, Rainmoon 2.** The stair, the sweater and the hug (c31-b5–c31-b10). "I'm so happy you're still alive, Zei."
