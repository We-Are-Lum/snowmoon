# Seed brief: The room change

> **Full spoilers** for chapters 2–10.

**The adaptation:** the chapter 2 bombing of Zei's Decentralized University (DU) physics class, told from the side of whoever moved the class at the last minute. Zei and Fin credit that person with saving their lives (c2-b124). Zei later reads the move as defense work (c9-b76).

**Source:** the committed chapter text in `content/snowmoon/text/`. Block IDs are `c<chapter>-b<idx>`. The bombing is on 3724 Snowmoon 5 in Pafogai Du, Dzego (c2-b1). Zei's later reflection is on 3724 Rainmoon 28 (c9-b1).

**What the text shows of this person.** It shows the messenger, not the decider:
- A man waits at the bottom of the stairs with the change of plans (c2-b35). He walks back up with Fin, Zei and a young woman, and sits down in the class with them (c2-b37). Zei remembers him as "the man who had redirected them last minute" (c2-b103).
- Whoever decided the move is never shown or named: "Whoever moved our room last-minute was a genius" (c2-b124).
- The text does not say whether the man and the decider are the same person. The adaptation can follow the messenger, the decider, or both.

- **The street:** Hun Min street in Pafogai Du. Low-rise electronics shops, tiny factories and stairways underground (c2-b3). A sensor shop beside a foil shop, under the sign "zui fia kun zun", "Underground safe place" (c2-b4–c2-b7). Trees shade the sidewalks (c2-b9).

## 2. What the text gives and what an adapter must invent

### Given

- The exact timeline:
  - 69,000: the location broadcast (c2-b22–c2-b24).
  - 1,000 ticks is about ten minutes (c2-b31).
  - Arrival "a few minutes later" (c2-b34).
  - 70,001: the door closes (c2-b41).
- The building's layout, as far as two floors and an "opposite room" (c2-b34–c2-b37).
- The messenger's single line and where he stood (c2-b35).
- That a young woman who knew Fin by name met them first (c2-b34).
- That the messenger and the woman then sat in the class (c2-b37).
- That the precautions were deliberate: new cryptography, sudden location changes, foil (c2-b104).
- That DU runs on anonymous cryptographic coordination (c2-b128).
- That Zei later reads the room change as "secretive, well-coordinated", "exactly that kind of work" as the defense work Den recruits him for (c9-b73, c9-b76).

### To invent

- **Who decided the move, and how.** The text says "whoever" (c2-b124).
- **What they knew, and when.** Whether there was a tip, a pattern, or a guess.
- **Why they moved the class one floor up instead of cancelling it or clearing the building,** given that the blast still broke the floor and injured Zei (c2-b99–c2-b100, c2-b112).
- **What the messenger knew when he said "Change of plans" (c2-b35),** and why he then sat in the class (c2-b37).
- **Whether anyone was in the "nearby room" (c2-b98), and whether it was the original room.**
- **Whether the decider is connected to the Kungaupei or Den.** The text links the act to "that kind of work" (c9-b76), not to a person.
- **Whether the decider ever learns of Zei's arm, or that Zei goes into defense** (c9-b76, c10-b194).
- **The young woman (c2-b34).** The text gives no name or role.
- **Whether anyone from Zei's DU chapter besides Fin was in the room.** Bai is in the same chapter (c5-b80), but the text does not place her there.

The seeded illustration `c2-b038-foil-classroom` (`content/snowmoon/illustrations/chapter-2.json`) shows the pig and the hamster holding "a plain blank banner". The text puts DU's motto on that poster (c2-b38–c2-b40).

## 5. Open creative questions

1. **Messenger, decider, or both?** Whose point of view is it: the man at the bottom of the stairs (c2-b35), the unseen "whoever" (c2-b124), or both? And does the adaptation keep them anonymous, as "grow roots, no head" coordination implies (c2-b128)?
2. **What did they know, and why only one floor?** Why move the class one floor up rather than cancel it, given that the move still left a boy with a broken arm (c2-b100, c2-b112)?
3. **Is it someone already in the book?** Possibilities include the young woman who knew Fin by name (c2-b34), someone in Bai's DU chapter (c5-b80), or the defense network behind Den (c9-b56, c9-b76). Or is it a stranger the book never meets? The text supports none of these over the others.

*Suggestion only.* A short story of 2,500–3,500 words, or an 8–10 minute audio piece.

7. **The blast.** Wall and floor (c2-b98–c2-b99); the boy's arm (c2-b100, c2-b109–c2-b112).

9. **Coda, Rainmoon 28.** The boy decides to do "exactly that kind of work" (c9-b76), whether or not the decider ever knows.
