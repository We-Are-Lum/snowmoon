# Licensing

Two licences, by what a file is (owner, 2026-10-22).

**The site's code: AGPL-3.0-only** (`LICENSE`). The application and its tools: `src/`, `scripts/`, `supabase/`,
`config/`, `public/` (except the book-derived media listed below), and the build files (`package.json`,
`next.config.ts`, `tsconfig.json`, `vercel.json`). The AGPL is the GPL plus one thing: if you run a modified copy
for others over a network, as a website, you must offer them its source too.

**The book and everything made from it: GPL-3.0-only** (`LICENSE-GPL-3.0`), the book's own licence. `content/`
(the book's text, narration, glossary, illustrations and recipes), `adaptations/`, `scenes-out/`,
`docs/source-figures/`, and every prompt, recipe and generated picture, voice or video published from this edition,
on the site or here. What people publish through the site is GPL-3.0 too (Terms, section 5).

The two fit together: section 13 of each licence allows combining GPL-3.0 and AGPL-3.0 work.

**Earlier versions.** Every version of the code up to this change was published under GPL-3.0, and stays available
under it from the repository's history.

**Contributing.** A contribution to the code is published under AGPL-3.0-only; one to content (a scene, a recipe,
media) under GPL-3.0-only. See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.
