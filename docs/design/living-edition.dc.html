Chapter 1
Gladias was walking along a foot path, with tall trees towering on both sides. A series of houses made out of large stone bricks lay behind the trees on both sides, medieval in style but without any of the junk, dust on the ground, or other imperfections that a real medieval structure would have had a thousand years earlier.
He looked around, breathed in and briefly stopped to enjoy the nature, dense enough and quiet enough that he almost forgot that he was in the middle of the Kalimar district of Meldan, the capital city of Veridia.
Suddenly, a small drone flew over his head. Gladias instinctively checked his watch. Almost immediately, a green checkmark lit up.
The drone flew off, and continued above a sidewalk branching off to the left. For a moment, Gladias could briefly see its shape: a small ball, with feather-like rotors on three sides.
"Five points for me!", the boy shouted excitedly.

DATA MODEL FLAGS · SCREENS 1 AND 2
6. Device templates only exist inside takes.Templates are human-authored elements, so they show only when the winning take includes them. On day one (2a) the vote screen is a bare table, and a take that leaves it out loses it. Suggest a base layer of renders that every take inherits unless it replaces one.
7. Images replace text with no way to choose otherwise.Rendering shows elements "in place of" their block range, so the book text disappears. 2b and 2c add a Book text toggle, but some creators will want the image above the text instead. Suggest anchors.display: replace | beside.
8. Anchor belongs to the element, not the version.anchors is keyed by element_id, so @ilse's v7 can't cover ¶ 11–16 if v1 covered ¶ 12–14. Changing the span means starting a new element and losing the version history.
9. No rule picks the narration.The play view assumes one house narration, but narrations has no default or score. Add a config entry for the house narration per chapter, or a scoring rule once there are alternatives.
10. Mixed attribution depends on takes collecting other people's work.2c shows 11 creators inside one take by @tovah. That works only because takes can include anyone's versions. Until section-by-section assembly ships, a chapter fills in only as fast as someone curates a take.
11. Block indices aren't paragraph numbers.The "¶" labels use idx, which counts headings, datelines and breaks. Readers will see gaps. A display number per readable block would fix it.
