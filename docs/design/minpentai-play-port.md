- **Tabs:** Learn, Play and Free play replace v3's four.

- **Addresses:**
  - `?mode=play`: the hub.
  - `?mode=free` or a `#fp=` board: free play.

  - `?mode=person` or `?invite=…`: one-on-one.

## 2. Tests

**`npm run test:minpentai-play`** runs Design's script from the prototype in `node:vm` beside the port and asserts identical results, 21,530 checks in all:
- the rules: `step()` on 400 random boards, 40 steps each;
- `botPlan` at every level under every new rule, on 240 positions;
- 48 whole matches (practice and every rung, every new rule) played by a scripted visitor, compared turn by turn: the board, the placements, "last seen", the history, the result, the ladder progress, and the screen's words and squares;
- the ladder screen at three progress states;
- 160 free play actions on phone, frame and desktop, the link written and read back;
- full screen on a phone and in the frame: the upright board, and aiming with arrows.

Planted divergences are caught: square hit points; the builder rung starting a level early; changed toast wording. A one-step change to the planner's look-ahead limit (40 to 39) is not caught, because no scripted match reaches it. That is the limit of a run-based comparison.

**`npm run test:minpentai-play-server`** (181 checks) covers:
- no view ever shows a hidden piece (a planted leak is caught);
- every way a placement can be refused;
- simultaneous turns resolve exactly as the prototype's flow;
- the clocks: 90 s setup, 45 s turns, starting after the run's frames;
- a pause after 15 s of silence, and resigning after 60 s more;
- leaving during setup cancels the match;
- a rematch needs both players within 20 s;
- the ladder's rules;
- each limit and each cleanup rule, just inside and just past its threshold (removing a limit makes its test fail).

**`npm run test:db`** (270 checks) applies 0009 and checks each table's privileges.

**`npm run check:minpentai-live`** (51 checks) plays one-on-one in two browsers at 390 × 844. The real server code runs against an in-memory database; only sign-in and the username lookup are stood in for. It covers:
- both players say they're ready, see each other by username, pick, and accept;
- setup, a run, and the next turn;
- each player is drawn as Cyan on the left;
- resign, rematch, and leaving during setup;
- an invite link, and blocking;
- signed out;
- a fog check on every match view (48 views, nothing hidden was sent);
- floors on every screen.

**`npm run check:ui`** now also covers the hub, free play, a practice match (place, end turn, the run), full screen at 390 and in the 424 × 695 frame, and `/minpentai/rules`. Free play is no longer v3's practice match.

## 3. Differences from Design

**Deliberate:**
- **Progress and sign-in are real.** Ladder progress comes from the server, readable only by you. Signed in or not is the site's sign-in. Design's mockup controls are left out: device switcher, sign-in toggle, sample progress, "opponent loses connection".
- **One-on-one is the owner's version, not Design's open queue.** Ready players are listed by username and you pick one; the picked player has 20 s to accept. There is also "OR SEND ONE PERSON A LINK", and blocking. The draft wording of D1 says who sees your name: signed-in players who are ready, and your opponent.
- **You are always Cyan,** as Design draws it. The server makes whoever accepts Amber; that player's board is turned half way round and the colours swapped, for drawing only.
- **The board fits its space.** Design's squares are 26 px (phone) and 44 px (desktop). They shrink where 15 squares don't fit: a 390 px phone, or the site's middle column beside the rail and the assistant.
- **Narrow desktop columns stack.** Where the middle column is under 900 px, the panel goes under the board.
- **Text floor:** the board's glyphs are at least 12 px (the "?" on a last-seen tower was 10 px).
- **Eight directions** wrap into two rows of four, so each button stays 44 px wide on a phone.
- **The board is keyboard reachable,** and each square says what is on it.
- **The Learn tab's text** says the lessons still use the first version of the rules. Design pointed to the v3 file.
- **The hub's "Play a person" tag** shows "SIGN IN" when signed out and nothing when signed in. Design's live "2 READY NOW" count is not shown on the hub; the lobby shows who is ready.
- **Desktop full screen** asks the browser for real full screen, and fills the window where that isn't allowed (A4).

**Not done:**
- Full screen inside lessons (A3); Learn is v3.
- Design's "room to grow" items (larger boards, four players).

### Usernames

The lobby, challenges and invites show the Farcaster username the server verified (`src/lib/names.ts`): 1 to 31 letters, numbers, dots or hyphens, the same rule as the lobby table's check in 0009. Farcaster's usernames all fit: an fname, or an ENS name whose label is at most 16 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, so the longest is a basename at 25 characters (docs.farcaster.xyz, "Usernames"). The check is not widened. If Farcaster ever returns a name that doesn't fit, the server refuses with "Your Farcaster username, "…", can't be shown in the lobby: usernames here are 1 to 31 letters, numbers, dots or hyphens. Playing the computer and free play still work." (400, never a server error; `test:minpentai-play-server` covers a 49-character `.base.eth` name and an emoji name, and fails without the fix).

### Privacy draft: what Play keeps

*Draft wording, for the privacy section.* Practice matches and free play keep nothing: they run in your browser, and a free play link holds the board in the link itself. Signed-in play keeps:

| What | What it holds | Who can see it | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ladder progress | Your FID, the highest rung open, and per rung the tries and the step of your first win | Only you | Until you ask for it to be erased |
| Blocks | Your FID and the FID you blocked | Only you (the other person isn't told) | Until you ask for it to be erased |
| The ready list | Your FID and your Farcaster username, while you say you're ready (3 minutes, renewed while the screen is open) | Other signed-in players who are ready | Deleted 1 hour after you stop being ready |
| Challenges | Who asked whom to play, and the answer | The two players | Deleted 1 hour after they're answered or run out |
| Invite links | The link, your FID and username, who used it | You, and whoever opens the link | Deleted when they run out, 24 hours after they're made |
| Match records | Both FIDs and usernames, the rule drawn, every move and every step (for "watch it again"), and the result | The two players only | Deleted 30 days after the match ends. A match nobody has opened for 24 hours ends with no result, and is deleted 30 days after that |
| Request counts | Per person, only this minute's and today's counts, for the limits | No one | Deleted the next day |
| Daily total | Requests to Play per day, with no person in it | No one | Deleted after 90 days |

Cleanup has no schedule of its own: any Play request runs it after answering, at most once every 10 minutes per server, up to 500 rows per table each time. An invite link that has expired and been cleaned up answers "This invite link is not valid."

### Limits (`MINPENTAI_PLAY` in src/lib/config.ts)

| Limit | Number | Answer past it |
|---|---|---|
| Requests per person per minute | 60 | 429 "Too many requests. Try again in a moment." with `retryAfterMs` and `Retry-After` |
| Requests per person per UTC day | 3,000 | the same 429 |
| One match's view, per person | at most once a second | the same 429; the page waits `retryAfterMs` |
| The lobby, per person | at most once every 2 seconds | the same 429 |
| All requests to Play, everyone, per UTC day | 200,000 | 503 "Play has reached today's limit. Practice and free play still work." |

Sign-in is checked first (401), then the database, then the limits. Every request counts toward the person's own limits; only those within them count toward the sitewide total, so one person at 429 does not spend everyone's budget.

There is no rating, ranking or leaderboard, no chat, and no record of anyone's matches shown to anyone but the two players.
