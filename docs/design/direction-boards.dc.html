DATA MODEL FLAGS · PART A
1. No element type for code-rendered set pieces.element_type allows design, text, image, clip. Boards, scoreboards, maps and device screens fit none. Suggest render with body {template, commit, state, viewport}; the 390px panel shows why viewport belongs in the body.
2. Tables lose their device.A table block is Markdown. It can't say watch vs hand device, which world, slider position, or check colour.
3. Lettering has no translation field.Needs text, lang, gloss, plus a layout: full | compact for long blocks.
4. Sections don't store a place.Analysis outputs a location per section; sections only has a title. The accent needs location_entity_id, and the location needs a world.
5. Posters won't ingest as quotes.Ephelion's texts in chapters 3 and 13 are a bold line, a rule, then plain paragraphs. Ingest will likely emit paragraph and break blocks, and the break will split a section in analysis. Needs a document kind or a hand-written block override.
