DECISIONS I GUESSED · MINPENTAI
  1. Full screen in Farcaster can't leave the frame, so it hides our chrome and turns the board upright. Squares go from 26 to 40 px.
  2. In full screen, a new glider is aimed by tapping one of four arrows around it on the board. That replaces the arrow row.
  3. Three tabs: Learn, Play, Free play. Practice, the ladder and one-on-one sit under Play.
  4. Free play replaces the sandbox. The book's cell rule and the c4-b5 board stay reachable from Under the hood. That changes the lessons' "the book's rule, in the sandbox" link and P8d.
  5. Free play allows placing only at step 0. Stepping back replays history, because the piece game isn't reversible.
  6. The ladder has five rungs: Rival, Blocker, Builder, Opportunist, Planner. Names, behaviours and the order are mine.
  7. A rung opens on one win; draws don't count. Progress is saved to your FID, readable only by you. There is no public record and no ranking.
  8. The ladder needs sign-in, as asked. Signed-out players keep practice and free play.
  9. One-on-one matches the next ready person, with no rating, so there's nothing to rank. A link for one named person is offered too.
  10. Turns to act are simultaneous and hidden in every mode. Timers are 90 s for setup and 45 s per turn.
  11. Disconnects pause the match for both players for 60 s, then count as resigning. Leaving during setup cancels the match with no result.
  12. A rematch needs both players within 20 s and draws a new rule.
  13. The setup countdown reuses the book's Dzegoban line (c4-b97–b98), as the broadcast does.
