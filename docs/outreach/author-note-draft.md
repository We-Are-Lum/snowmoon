Hi Vitalik,

I'm building an open, illustrated and narrated edition of *Snowmoon* as a
Farcaster miniapp. Following your note on the license, the whole pipeline is
public under GPL-3.0: https://github.com/We-Are-Lum/snowmoon. That includes
the scripts, every prompt, and a recipe for each generated audio block and
image.

So far there is a synthetic narration of all 32 chapters (an open-weights
model with a stock voice, no cloned voices) and a first set of illustrations,
all clearly labeled as an unofficial adaptation. There is no token, and nothing
is sold.

Two questions:

1. We'd like to publish the narration as a free podcast feed, so people can
   listen in Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other apps, with the GPL notice and the
   source link in every episode and the files also downloadable without DRM. Are
   you comfortable with that?
2. Is there a pronunciation you have in mind for Dzegoban, and for names like
   Gladias, Seila and Deluin? We applied one consistent rule
   (content/snowmoon/pronunciation.json), but we'd happily use yours.

If anything here isn't how you'd want the book treated, tell me and I'll change
it.

Thanks for writing it and for releasing it the way you did.
