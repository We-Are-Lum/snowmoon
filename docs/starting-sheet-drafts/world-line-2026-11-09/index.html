Does a world line make remixes feel like Snowmoon’s world? Twelve everyday prompts, two per character, each from the character’s base front, without and with this line, inserted right after the scene line of the route’s own prompt:

Set it in Snowmoon's world: quiet technology woven into old stone, castle towers, mountains and gardens. Show this world's own version of anything mentioned, like its games, sports, clothes, vehicles and buildings.

FLUX and GPT Image 2.5 on all 12; Gemini on cases 1, 5, 8 and 11 (cases 3 and 10 didn’t fit the cap). FLUX uses one seed for both; GPT and Gemini take no seed, so their pairs also differ by chance. Then a GPT-only follow-up with a book-based line (section 2). The marks are the coding agent’s reading, strict on skin tone. All prompts passed the prompt check.

The project’s own test pictures: not published. Drafted by the coding agent (Claude, a closed model by Anthropic). DRAFT until the owner approves it.
